    About

    I'm an Electrical Engineering student at Zakir Husain College of Engineering and Technology, Aligarh Muslim University, grounded in real-time control systems and signal processing.

    At Orbitalink I lead the development of antenna tracking systems end to end: a 435 MHz Yagi-Uda antenna, a Fusion 360 tracker chassis, firmware for hybrid servo drives, and adaptive control for a high-inertia mount.

    At IIT Ropar I modelled a five-phase IPMSM, designed field-oriented control for it, and generated ePWM on a TI DSP to test the gate driver. I'm drawn to aerospace applications and generative AI, and want to keep building where hardware and control meet.
