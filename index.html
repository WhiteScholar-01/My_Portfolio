01 / ABOUT

Where control, hardware and RF meet

A control law that works in Simulink and one that works on a real motor are not the same thing. Most of my work lives in the gap between them.

At Orbitalink I lead the antenna tracker: a 435 MHz cross-Yagi, a Fusion 360 chassis stiff enough to hold a beam on a moving satellite, motion firmware for hybrid servo drives, and adaptive control for a mount heavy enough to fight back.

At IIT Ropar I built a five-phase IPMSM drive from the transforms up — two current planes instead of one, five regulators instead of three, and a torque equation I had to derive before I could use it. Then I put the modulator on a TI C2000 DSP and tested the gate driver.

Electrical Engineering at Zakir Husain College of Engineering and Technology, AMU.
