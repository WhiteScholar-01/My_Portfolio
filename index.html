About me

I'm an Electrical Engineering student at Zakir Husain College of Engineering and Technology, AMU, working where control systems, power electronics and RF meet.

At Orbitalink I lead antenna tracking systems end to end: a 435 MHz cross-Yagi antenna, a Fusion 360 chassis, motion firmware for hybrid servo drives, and adaptive control for a high-inertia mount.

At IIT Ropar I modelled a five-phase IPMSM, designed field-oriented control with loss-aware MTPA, and generated ePWM on a TI DSP to test the gate driver.
